Clinical Explanations

The rules below are educational pattern-recognition rules derived from the supplied laboratory rule table and blood-film morphology SOP.

Microcytosis

Microcytosis with anaemia, low MCH, hypochromia, pencil cells and target cells can be associated with iron deficiency. Ferritin and iron studies may be considered.

Macrocytosis

Macrocytosis may occur with B12/folate deficiency, liver disease, medication effects, alcohol-related disease, reticulocytosis or marrow disorders. Morphology and clinical information should be considered.

Thrombocytopenia

A platelet count below 150 ×10⁹/L meets the supplied rule definition for thrombocytopenia. Platelet clumping should be considered when the result is unexpectedly low.

Thrombocytosis

A platelet count above 450 ×10⁹/L meets the supplied rule definition for thrombocytosis. Reactive causes such as infection, inflammation and iron deficiency should be considered.

Neutropenia

The supplied morphology SOP highlights infection, medication effects, liver disease, viral illness, marrow disorders and other causes. Persistent or severe neutropenia may warrant professional review.

Neutrophilia

Neutrophilia with toxic changes can occur with infection or inflammation. A marked neutrophilia with myelocytes, eosinophilia or basophilia may require specialist investigation.

Possible haemolysis

Anaemia with increased bilirubin and appropriate morphology may suggest haemolysis. Reticulocyte count, DAT and clinical correlation may be required.

Malaria

Where malaria is clinically suspected, travel history and appropriate blood-film/parasitological testing are important. Positive or strongly suspected cases require urgent professional handling.

Important

The application does not establish a diagnosis. Laboratory results must be interpreted using the patient's clinical history, local laboratory reference intervals and appropriate confirmatory testing.

About Blood Test AI

Blood Test AI is a rule-based educational clinical reasoning interface. It accepts a broad set of haematology and biochemical parameters and applies structured rules to identify patterns.

  • Red-cell indices

  • Renal markers

  • Inflammatory markers
